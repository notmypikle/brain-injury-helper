# Monetization Plan — B.I.H.

*B.I.H. serves people with brain injuries — a vulnerable audience. Rule #1:
monetization must never exploit the user. Ethical pricing is the brand.*

## PRINCIPLES
1. **Core safety and daily-help features stay free** — crisis support, notes, reminders, routines are never paywalled.
2. **No ads, ever** — ads in a cognitive-support tool are harmful and erode trust.
3. **No data selling, ever** — privacy is the product.
4. **Price for sustainability, not extraction** — cover costs + fund development.

## TIERS (draft)

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Chat (text + voice), notes, reminders, routines, crisis support, local storage |
| **Plus** | $4.99/mo or $49/yr | Smarter AI (better model), paperwork helper, encrypted cloud backup, unlimited history export |
| **Caregiver** | $9.99/mo | Plus features + shared view for a family member/caregiver (with the user's explicit consent), appointment coordination |

## WHY THIS WORKS
- **Free tier is genuinely useful** → trust, word of mouth in brain-injury communities (TBI associations, rehab centers, Reddit/Facebook groups).
- **Plus is cheap vs. alternatives** — human cognitive-assistant tools cost far more; $5/mo is accessible.
- **Caregiver tier** solves a real pain: families coordinating care. People pay for peace of mind.

## B2B PATH (bigger long-term revenue)
- **Rehab clinics & occupational therapists**: license B.I.H. as a take-home support tool ($/seat/month). Pilot with 2–3 clinics before building dashboards.
- **Grant funding**: brain-injury nonprofits and health-innovation grants fund assistive tech. Apply once MVP is live with real users.

## COST SIDE
- AI is the main variable cost — use the per-message cost model (see companion project's cost-modeling doc for the method). Budget model on Free, better model on Plus.
- Voice is free: on-device STT/TTS (see docs/VOICE_AND_PRODUCTION.md).
- Target: Free user ≈ $0.30/mo cost, Plus revenue covers ~15 free users each.

## LAUNCH SEQUENCE
1. Free MVP on Play Store → grow users + collect feedback
2. Plus tier once AI quality justifies it
3. Caregiver tier after consent/sharing is built carefully (privacy-sensitive)
4. B2B pilots after consumer traction proves the product
